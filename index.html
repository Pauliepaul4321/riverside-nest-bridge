Riverside Nest Bridge

A private household integration for connecting authorised Google Nest thermostats to Apple Home through Homebridge.

This is a personal household project, not a commercial app. It has no public sign-up, account registration or subscription service.

Setup status: Nest is not yet connected; the bridge is still being set up. Thermostat controls through this bridge have not yet been verified.

What the bridge is designed to do

Once connected, and with the household owner’s permission, the bridge will use Google Nest’s Smart Device Management service to read supported thermostat information and make permitted temperature and mode changes.

  • Display temperature, humidity, thermostat mode, target temperature and heating or idle state, where available.
  • Use device and room metadata to identify the authorised thermostats.
  • Allow authorised Apple Home users to change supported thermostat modes and temperature setpoints through HomeKit.

How the connection works

The intended setup runs Homebridge on the household owner’s always-on Mac. Once connected, it will communicate with Google’s Nest service and receive device updates through Google cloud event delivery. Homebridge will present supported thermostat controls to Apple Home using HomeKit.

The integration depends on those services as well as the local bridge. Google and Apple operate their respective services; Riverside Nest Bridge is an independent personal integration.

Privacy and access

The owner chooses which supported Nest devices to authorise. Access can be withdrawn through the relevant Google Account or Partner Connections permissions, and the local bridge can be disabled.

Read the privacy and data-handling notice for the information used, credential storage and revocation details.
