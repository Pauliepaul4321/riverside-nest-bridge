Privacy and data handling

This notice describes the intended data handling for Riverside Nest Bridge, a private Nest-to-Apple-Home integration operated by its household owner. It is not a commercial app and has no public sign-ups.

Setup status: Nest is not yet connected; the bridge is still being set up. This notice describes the information and controls intended for use once the connection is authorised and working.

Information the bridge will use

After the owner authorises access, the bridge will use Google Nest’s Smart Device Management service to work with supported thermostats and their available data:

  • Measured temperature and humidity.
  • Thermostat mode, temperature setpoints, and heating or idle state.
  • Device and room metadata needed to identify and display the authorised thermostats.
  • Google authorisation credentials needed to maintain the permitted connection.

The available information and controls depend on the thermostat and the permissions granted. The owner’s Google account password is entered with Google, not into these information pages.

Purpose and data flow

Once connected, the information will be used to show thermostat status and support temperature and mode changes in the household’s Apple Home setup. Commands will pass through Homebridge on the owner’s always-on Mac to Google’s Nest service. Google cloud event delivery will provide device updates to the bridge.

Homebridge will make supported thermostat information and controls available through HomeKit to authorised Apple Home users and controllers. Google and Apple operate their respective cloud and home services, with their own data handling and account settings. This integration is not wholly local.

Storage, backups and diagnostics

The intended setup stores OAuth credentials and bridge configuration in a protected local Homebridge folder on the owner’s Mac. Access to that folder is restricted through the Mac’s account and file permissions. Credentials are not part of these public-facing information pages.

Homebridge and its components may produce operational diagnostic logs. Those logs can contain device information or other sensitive details and must remain private; they must not be published. Logging, cached data and retention depend on the installed components and local settings. The owner may keep private backups of credentials, configuration or logs in local or cloud storage. Those copies have their own access and retention settings and are not part of this public website.

Stored configuration and credentials remain until the owner removes them, subject to any backup retention. Google, Apple, Homebridge and its components may keep logs or telemetry under their respective policies and settings.

Withdrawing access and removing the bridge

  1. Withdraw the relevant permissions in Google Account connections and/or the Nest Partner Connections permissions for this integration.
  2. Disable or remove the Nest bridge in Homebridge to stop local operation.
  3. If desired, remove the bridge’s locally stored credentials, configuration and diagnostic logs, including any retained backups the owner controls.
  4. Remove the corresponding bridge or accessories from Apple Home if they are no longer needed.

Removing local files does not itself revoke Google permissions. Revoking access does not delete data retained separately by Google or Apple under their own service policies.

This information website

These static information pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts, advertising scripts or external embedded resources, and the page code does not set cookies. They do not provide access to the household’s devices or credentials.
